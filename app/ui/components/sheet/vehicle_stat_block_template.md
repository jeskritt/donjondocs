> ### Properties
> ***Sealed & Pressurized.*** Occupants are protected from hard vacuum and hostile atmospheres.
>
> ***Sensor Array.*** The vehicle has advantage on checks made to detect other vehicles.
>
> ### Armament
> ***Heavy Machine Gun (x2).*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) piercing damage.
>
> ***Missile Pod (Recharge 5-6).*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 1 mile, one target. *Hit:* 27 (5d10) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity save or be knocked prone.
>
> ### Actions
> ***Ram.*** The vehicle moves up to its speed toward a creature or object and makes one attack: *+7 to hit*. *Hit:* 14 (4d6) bludgeoning damage.
>
> ***Evasive Maneuvers.*** Until the start of its next turn, attack rolls against the vehicle have disadvantage while it is moving.
